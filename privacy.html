FishSort Level Editor – Privacy

The FishSort Level Editor is an internal tool of RiseOn Game Studio. Its designers use it to lay out the levels of the game Fish Sort, which are kept in one Google Sheet of the studio.

What it reads and writes

Without signing in, the editor reads that level sheet, which is shared for viewing. When you save, change or delete a level, it asks you to sign in with Google and, with your permission (Google Sheets), writes that level's tab of the same sheet. Google only lets it write if your account may edit the sheet. It does not open any other file of yours.

What it keeps

The sign-in token stays in the open browser page and is gone when the page closes. The editor stores no personal data, sends nothing to any server of ours or of anyone else besides Google's own sign-in and Sheets services, and has no analytics or ads. Your email address is only shown in the editor, so you can see which account you signed in with.
